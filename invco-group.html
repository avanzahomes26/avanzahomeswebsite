Our Origin

Every Empire Starts in a Garage.

INVCO Group didn't start as a company. It started as a decision.

In 2016, Anderson Lizarazo and his wife made a bet, the kind of bet you only make when you believe in something more than the risk it carries. They founded Investment Construction LLC in Florida, walked away from the safe path, and started building.

The first projects were in Washington State. Small. Unproven. The kind of jobs that don't make it into portfolios, but the kind that teach you everything.

From there, they moved deeper into Florida. Umatilla. Fruitland Park. Fort Meade. Cobb. DeLand. Every project taught them something new: how to acquire land, how to entitle it, how to build it, how to sell it. And every project, they got better.

That's how INVCO Group grew. Not through a marketing plan. Not through venture capital. Through the same thing that built every real company in history, showing up, taking the hit, and doing it better the next time.

Today, INVCO Group is a full-service construction and development company operating across Florida, with active projects in Charlotte County, North Port, Highlands County (Sebring), Citrus County, Palm Bay, and Kissimmee. It has served over 450 families, executed 100+ property transactions, and developed 350+ lots.

Avanza Homes, the brand most people know us by, was born from INVCO Group. So was the mentorship. So was the conference. So was everything.

But it all started with two people who decided to build.
