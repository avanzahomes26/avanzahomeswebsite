Meet Your Mentor

From Immigrant to Developer. No Shortcuts. No Handouts.

Anderson Lizarazo arrived in the United States at 17 years old without speaking a word of English. No connections. No capital. No roadmap.

He started at the bottom, working labor jobs while learning the language and the business. His first venture into real estate was house flipping, buying distressed properties, renovating them, and selling for a profit. Within a few years, he was running 3 to 5 flips simultaneously.

But he saw the ceiling. Flipping doesn't scale. The margins compress, the labor never stops, and one bad deal can erase months of work.

So he made the pivot that changed everything: land development.

Instead of buying someone else's problem and trying to fix it, he started buying raw land and building from the ground up. His first land development deal became his first real home run. From there, he built the system, the team, and the track record that became Avanza Homes.

Today, Anderson is the CEO and founder of Avanza Homes, a Florida-licensed general contractor that has delivered over 120 new construction homes across three high-growth markets. He's developed and entitled more than 350 lots, helped 450+ families, and hosts his own annual real estate conference, the Avanza Conference, with 700+ attendees and a lineup of multiple industry speakers.

He's also been a featured speaker at multiple real estate conferences across the country, sharing the exact strategies he used to go from zero to building 20+ homes per year.

His mentorship isn't theory. It's the system he uses every single day.
